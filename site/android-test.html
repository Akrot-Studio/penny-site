Android test

Help bring Penny to Google Play.

Before a new app can go public, Google asks for a small group of people to try it for two weeks. Join them and use Penny on your phone before anyone else: know what's safe to spend today, with spending added from your bank alerts and receipts, and no bank login.

Leave your email

Use the email of the Google account on your phone, the one you use for the Play Store. That's how Google lets you in.

Get the invite

When the test opens, we email you a link. Tap it, accept the invite, and install Penny from the Play Store like any other app.

Keep it for 14 days

Use it as much or as little as you like, just keep it installed for two weeks. Tell us what you think, if you want to, on Discord or by email.

Join the test

Questions

Is it safe to test?

Yes. It's the same Penny that's on the Microsoft Store: your data is encrypted on your phone, there are no Penny servers and no analytics, and Sync between your devices is optional, end-to-end encrypted, and uses your own Google Drive. Reading bank alerts is optional and off until you turn it on.

Will I lose my data when the test ends?

No. When Penny goes public, the app simply updates. Everything you entered stays.

Do I have to give feedback?

No, but we'd love it. The Discord is the quickest way, or write to support@akrot.app.
