Protections

Layers, not promises.

Each protection works on its own. Together they cover a lost phone, a shared computer and a bad import.

Encrypted at rest

Your data file is encrypted with AES-256. The key is sealed by Windows for your user account, or by your phone's secure hardware.

Passcode and recovery key

Add a passcode for a second lock. A recovery key gets you back in if you forget it, and only you hold it.

Privacy mode

One switch hides every amount on screen, for budgeting on the train or sharing your screen. Try it.

Verified backups

Backups every day and before big changes, each one checked, so a bad import is one click to undo.

Notifications, narrowly

Off until you turn it on and pick your bank apps. Only purchases from those apps are kept, encrypted. Codes and balances never are.

Works offline

Penny doesn't need the internet. Nothing about you or your money is sent anywhere; checking for updates can be switched off.
