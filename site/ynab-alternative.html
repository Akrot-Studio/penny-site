What planning looks like in Penny

  • Payday plan: when your pay lands, Penny suggests how to split it between bills, goals, a cushion and everyday money, then tracks the plan.
  • Safe to spend today: instead of checking a dozen category balances, one number tells you what you can spend without missing a bill.
  • Set-asides for costs that come back: car insurance, gifts, yearly subscriptions, saved for a little each month.
  • A safety buffer in three steps, so the first goal is reachable, not overwhelming.
  • A cash-flow calendar that marks the tight days before you get there.

Bring your YNAB history

Export your register from YNAB, then open Import in Penny and choose the file. Penny recognises YNAB's columns, sets up your accounts, matches your categories and skips duplicates. Undo is one click.
