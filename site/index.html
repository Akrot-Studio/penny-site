    Spent Safe to spend today

    Kept on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Bank statements are written for machines.

    Download the file from your bank's website and drop it into Penny. It recognizes the bank, tidies every payee, files it in the right category and records card payments as transfers, not spending.

    What your bank sends
    What Penny shows

    Recognized automatically: Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Capital One, Citi, American Express, Discover, Apple Card, U.S. Bank and Ally, plus Mint, YNAB and Monarch exports. Any other CSV, OFX or QFX file works too.

    Your money stays on your PC.

    Penny never asks for your bank password and has no servers to send your data to. Turn on privacy mode and every number disappears, on this page too.

    No bank login

    Penny reads the files you download yourself. It never connects to your bank.

    Encrypted on disk

    AES-256, tied to your Windows account. Add a passcode and a recovery key for a second lock.

    Backed up for you

    Verified backups every day and before big changes, so a bad import is one click to undo.

    One look at today, and the rest when you want it.
