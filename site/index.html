    Privacy

    Nobody sees your money. Not even us.

    Penny never asks for your bank password and has no servers to send your data to. Privacy mode hides every number at once. Try it on this page.

    Penny reads the files you download and the alerts your bank already sends you. It never connects to your bank.

    Questions, answered.

    The things people ask before they install a money app. Short answers, no fine print.

    Does Penny connect to my bank?

    No. Penny never asks for a bank password and never logs in anywhere. On Android it can read the purchase alerts your bank app already shows you; on any device you can import a statement (CSV, OFX or QFX). Major US banks are recognized automatically.

    Where is my data, and who can see it?

    Only on your device, encrypted with AES-256 and sealed by Windows or your phone's secure hardware. There is no account and no cloud, and Penny has no servers. Add a passcode for a second lock, with a recovery key in case you forget it.

    Which languages and currencies does it speak?

    English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Hungarian, Turkish, Russian, Indonesian, Chinese, Hindi and Arabic, with right-to-left layout for Arabic. Bank alerts, quick entry and Ask Penny understand all 13. About 40 currencies, with accounts in different currencies converted to your main one.

    Is it really free?

    Yes, everything is free during the beta. A Pro plan will add extras later, with a free week to try it; everyday budgeting stays free, and data you already have is never removed or locked away.

    How is the health score worked out?

    Four pillars (Spend, Save, Borrow, Plan) scored 0 to 100 with the Penny Research model, built from the latest household-finance research and tested on realistic households.

    What if I already use Mint, YNAB or Monarch?

    Export from your old app and import the file. Penny recognizes those exports with all their accounts, keeps transfers as transfers and skips duplicates.

    Which devices does Penny run on?

    Windows 10 and 11 today, and Android is coming to Google Play. iPhone and Mac may follow; get the newsletter to hear first.

    Is it safe to let Penny read notifications?

    It's off until you turn it on and pick your bank apps. Penny checks those notifications on your phone for a purchase, keeps only the ones from apps you picked (encrypted), and never sends them anywhere. Codes and balances are never kept.
