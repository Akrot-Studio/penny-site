2.3.0November 10, 2026Latest

Sync between your devices

  • Your PC and your phone, in step. Turn on Sync in Settings, sign in with Google once, and link your other devices with a QR code. A change on one shows up on the others.
  • Encrypted before it leaves your device. Penny keeps the copies in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, sealed with a key only your linked devices have. Not Google, and not us, can read them. Penny still has no servers and no account.
  • Off until you turn it on. Without Sync, nothing changes: everything stays on your device.
2.2.0November 10, 2026

Every currency in the world, and a steadier Android app

Penny now has one version number everywhere: the app, the Microsoft Store and Google Play all say 2.2.0.

  • All 155 active currencies. Including the Hungarian forint (Ft). Search by name, code or symbol, in your language. Amounts use each currency's own decimals: none for the yen, three for the Kuwaiti dinar.
  • Language and currency on the welcome screen. Penny suggests your currency from your phone or PC, and you can change both later in Settings › Language & money.
  • Changing your currency never changes your amounts. Penny asks whether your accounts stay in the old currency or were really in the new one.
  • Android opens reliably again. After allowing notifications, Penny could become slow to open or stop opening. Reminders now update only what changed, and nothing about notifications can hold Penny up when it starts.
  • Tapping the tab you're on does nothing. Your place on the page, your search and your filters stay put.
1.1.1November 6, 2026

A fix for Windows

  • Penny opens again on Windows. After updating to 1.1.0, Penny on Windows could open to an empty green window. This update fixes it. Your data was never touched and is all still there.
  • 13 languages in the Microsoft Store. The Store now lists all 13 languages Penny speaks.
1.1.0November 6, 2026

Unlock with your face or fingerprint, in 13 languages

  • Windows Hello and fingerprint unlock. Open Penny with your face, fingerprint or PIN on Windows, and with your fingerprint or face on Android.
  • Four more languages. German, Italian, Hungarian and Turkish.
  • Shop logos. Optional logos next to well-known shops in your transactions. Off by default.
  • More currencies. Exchange rates for many more currencies.
  • Fixes. The Alarms & reminders prompt on Android no longer keeps coming back, and the Health report tab lines up with the other Insights tabs.
1.0.0October 31, 2026

The first public version of Penny

  • Know what's safe to spend. One number a day until your next payday, after every bill, card payment and set-aside. Tap the (?) to see every step.
  • Your money, written down for you. Bank alerts and receipts on Android, plain-language entry in nine languages, and bank statements from many countries.
  • Plan the money you have. A payday plan, a safety buffer in three steps, set-asides for costs that come back, budgets by month or by paycheck, goals and a cash-flow calendar.
  • Insights that explain. Why spending moved, overlapping subscriptions, honest debt plans, a Financial Health Report, a weekly review and a year in review.
  • Private by design. Encrypted on your device, with a passcode and recovery key. No account, no bank login, no ads, no trackers.
