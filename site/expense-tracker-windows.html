For Windows

An expense tracker that lives on your PC, not in the cloud.

Penny is a real Windows app: it works offline, keeps your money data encrypted on your computer, and never asks for a bank login. Free from the Microsoft Store for Windows 10 and 11.

Get it from MicrosoftSee what it does

Made for the desktop

  • Desktop widget: today's safe-to-spend number, your budgets, what's coming up and a quick-add box, always on screen.
  • Windows notifications for bills and reminders, even when Penny is in the tray.
  • Bank statements in seconds: drop in the CSV, OFX or QFX file from your bank's website. Messy descriptions become clean names and duplicates are skipped.
  • Keyboard first: Ctrl+K to search or ask Penny a question; add a purchase by typing “coffee 4.50 yesterday” and it's filed.
  • Export to Excel whenever you like. Your data is never locked in.
  • Looks at home: light and dark themes that can follow your Windows accent colour.

Private, properly

  • Your data is encrypted with AES-256, tied to your Windows account. Add a passcode and a recovery key for a second lock.
  • No account, no servers, no analytics, no ads. Nothing about your money leaves your PC.
  • Automatic, verified backups, and privacy mode hides every amount with one click.

More than a list of expenses

Penny tells you what's safe to spend today after the bills still to come, plans each paycheck, shows tight days ahead on a cash-flow calendar, and ranks insights by the money at stake: price rises, double charges, free trials about to end. See every feature.
