Your first five minutes

From install to your first plan.

Penny opens with a short welcome that asks only what it needs.

  1. Pick your currency and language

    Then add an account or two: the cash in your wallet, your checking account, a card.

  2. Bring in your spending

    Import a statement from your bank, or on Android turn on bank alerts. Or simply type what you bought.

  3. Read your number

    Overview shows what's safe to spend today. Add budgets and goals whenever you're ready.

Will Penny always be free?

Everything is free during the beta. A Pro plan will add extras later, with a free week to try it. Everyday budgeting stays free, and data you already have is never removed or locked away.

Can I use Penny on my PC and my phone?

Yes, Penny runs on both, and each device keeps its data encrypted on that device.

Why the Microsoft Store?

Store apps are checked and signed by Microsoft, install in one click, and update by themselves.
